[[LG-03-12]]
==== LG 03-12: Business and technical views (R3)

Software architects know that the distinction between business (aka _domain_) and technical aspects or perspectives is a useful prerequisites for designing systems.

* **Business perspectives** are often created or clarified first, as they help to identify missing requirements and clarify scope.

** External actors: users, partner organizations, regulatory bodies, adjacent business systems
** Relationships expressed in domain/business terms: data flows, service dependencies, business events

** The target audience are product owners, business stakeholders, domain experts.


* **Technical perspectives** contain structural or crosscutting decisions on, for example:

** Technology stacks, specific frameworks, libraries or other products used in development or operation of systems
** Technical channels/protocols used for communication
** Protocols (REST, AMQP, JDBC), message formats, authentication mechanisms
** Techncial infrastructure
** Target audience: developers, architects, infrastructure teams

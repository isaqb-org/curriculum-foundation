[[LG-05-04]]
==== LG 05-04: Understand the importance of specification quality and verification in AI-generated code and agentic systems (R1)

* Architects understand that when delegating tasks to AI agents, the consequences of implicit assumptions are amplified: agents interpret specifications literally and may act on ambiguous, incomplete, or contradictory instructions in ways a human would not.   
* Architects understand that good specifications contain unambiguous, general descriptions of the desired behavior - not merely examples - and the cost of ambiguity is higher in AI-driven development.   
* Architects can write explicit specifications of constraints, edge cases, forbidden actions, and escalation paths, and treat this as an architectural responsibility (see also LG 04-06)  
* Architects understand that verification of AI-generated output is a first-class architectural activity, because AI-generated output may be incorrect in ways that are not obvious. Verification methods include using statistical, scenario-based, formal methods, and adversarial approaches.

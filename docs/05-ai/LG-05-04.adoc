[[LG-05-04]]
==== LG 05-04: Specification quality and verification in AI-generated artifacts (R1-R3)

* Architects understand that good specifications contain unambiguous, general descriptions of the desired behavior - not merely examples. (R1)
* Architects can write explicit specifications of constraints, edge cases, forbidden actions, and escalation paths, and treat this as an architectural responsibility. (see also LG 04-06) (R2)
* Architects understand that verification of AI-generated output is a first-class architectural activity, because AI-generated output may be incorrect in ways that are not obvious. Verification methods include statistical, scenario-based, and formal methods. (R1)
* Architects understand the risks of having an LLM review its own output. (R3)

[[LG-05-06]]
==== LG 05-06: Recognize the ethical and societal externalities of AI (R3)

* Architects understand that choosing AI infrastructure is an architectural decision with societal impact.  
* Architects understand the externalities of AI infrastructure: energy/CO~2~, labor-market pressure, disruption of open-source and internet ecosystems, model bias in public infrastructure, political destabilization.

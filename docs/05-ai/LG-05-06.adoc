[[LG-05-06]]
==== LG 05-06: Recognize the risks, ethical and societal externalities of AI (R2, R3)

* Architects know that AI introduces IP/legal risks and risks of degrading critical skills and disrupting learning processes, security risks, and creates dependencies on a small number of providers. (R2)
* Architects understand the societal externalities of AI infrastructure: energy/CO~2~, labor-market pressure, disruption of open-source and internet ecosystems, model bias in public infrastructure, political destabilization. (R3)

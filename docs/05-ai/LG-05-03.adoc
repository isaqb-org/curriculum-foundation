[[LG-05-03]]
==== LG 05-03: Understand the impact on quality of AI-generated code and agentic systems (R1) 

* Architects know that AI-generated code tends to be of lower quality than human-written code, carries model-inherent risks (hallucination, sensitivity to prompt structure), and requires critical human review.  
* Architects understand that the scope and granularity of a unit of work delegated to an AI materially affects the quality of the outcome.

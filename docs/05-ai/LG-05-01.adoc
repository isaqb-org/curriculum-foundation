[[LG-05-01]]
==== LG 05-01: Appropriateness of AI-based systems (R1-R2)

* Architects can decide when using AI is appropriate for the task at hand. (R2)
* Architects understand that when delegating tasks to AI agents, the consequences of implicit assumptions are amplified: agents may act on ambiguous, incomplete, or contradictory instructions in ways a human would not. (R1)

[[LG-05-01]]
==== LG 05-01: Distinguish symbolic and stochastic AI, the role of LLMs and their architectural implications (R1)

* Architects can explain the difference between symbolic (deterministic, rule-based, explainable) and stochastic (probabilistic, learned, opaque, training-data and bias-sensitive) AI and LLMs.
* Architects understand the basic mode of operation of an LLM, which processes a prompt together with its context and is stateless across calls.
  It does not retain memory of previous interactions unless that history is included in the context.
* Architects can decide when using AI is appropriate for the task at hand, and can decide which kind of AI is appropriate.

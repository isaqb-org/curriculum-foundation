== Use and Risks of Artificial Intelligence in Software Architectures

=== Purpose

The purpose of this section is to give training participants a foundational understanding of using artificial intelligence to support architecture development.
The section covers the impact of AI-generated artifacts and agentic engineering on quality, the importance of specification quality and verification of AI-generated output, the systemic risks of AI, and its ethical and societal externalities.
More extensive coverage of AI-assisted architecture is in Advanced Level module AGENTA <<isaqbagenta>>.
The architecture of AI-based systems is covered by Advanced Level module SWARC4AI <<isaqbswarc4ai>>.

We expect at least some of the content to move to existing learning goals.

=== Relevant Terms

== Use and Risks of Artificial Intelligence in Software Architectures

=== Purpose

The purpose of this section is to give training participants a foundational understanding of artificial intelligence as an architectural concern.
Participants learn to distinguish symbolic from stochastic AI, understand the role of LLMs and AI agents, and are able to decide when and which kind of AI is appropriate for a given task.
The section covers the impact of AI-generated code and agentic engineering on quality, the importance of specification quality and verification of AI-generated output, the systemic risks of AI, and its ethical and societal externalities.
The design and control of AI agents themselves are covered by the Advanced Level modules AGENTA and SWARC4AI.

We expect at least some of the content to move to existing learning goals.

=== Relevant Terms

Symbolic AI, Stochastic AI, LLM, AI Agent, AI, Assistant, Agentic Engineering, AI-generated output

# 016 - LG 05-03 on AI Externalities Is a Deliberate Exception to ADR 011

## Status: Accepted

## Context

- ADR [011](011-socio-technical-system-out-of-scope.md) treats socio-technical systems as out of scope for the Foundation Level curriculum, mainly because the curriculum is already at capacity and such topics open wide fields of discussion.
- The new section 5 "Use and Risks of Artificial Intelligence" (PR #852, issue #824) contains LG 05-03 "Recognize the risks, ethical and societal externalities of AI", which covers societal externalities of AI infrastructure (energy, labor market, open-source ecosystems, model bias, political destabilization).
- During review it was pointed out that some of the reasoning of ADR 011 also applies to this learning goal.
- Including a learning goal on the ethical and societal aspects of AI is a directive of the iSAQB Strategy Council, originally directed at the AI-related Advanced Level curricula. The Foundation Level Working Group (FLWG) agrees with this directive.

## Decision

LG 05-03 is a deliberate and narrow exception to ADR 011. ADR 011 remains in force for all other topics.

## Rationale

- The Strategy Council explicitly asked for coverage of ethical and societal aspects of AI in iSAQB curricula.
- The learning goal is restricted to naming risks (R2) and describing externalities (R3). It does not introduce socio-technical theory, feedback loops, or organizational models, which were the main concerns of ADR 011.
- The additional teaching time is small: the whole section 5 is budgeted at 60 minutes.

## Consequences

- LG 05-03 stays in the curriculum with R2/R3 relevance only.
- The exception does not justify further learning goals on socio-technical systems. Any such proposal still has to argue against ADR 011.
- If the Strategy Council directive changes, this ADR and LG 05-03 are to be revisited.

## Alternatives Considered

- Drop LG 05-03 and refer to the Advanced Level modules AGENTA and SWARC4AI only. Rejected because it contradicts the Strategy Council directive.
- Supersede ADR 011. Rejected because the reasoning of ADR 011 is still valid for the general case.
